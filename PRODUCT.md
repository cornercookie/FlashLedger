# FlashLedger PRODUCT.md

## Product Vision
FlashLedger (隨身帳) is a portable desktop accounting application designed for privacy-conscious users who want lightning-fast local bookkeeping without cloud sync, subscriptions, or login walls. All data resides in a local SQLite database next to the executable.

## Target Audience
- Users wanting absolute data ownership and offline reliability.
- People managing personal finances on portable USB drives or local workstations.

## Core Workflows
1. **Quick Entry**: Instantly record income/expense with date, amount, account, category, and optional notes.
2. **Monthly Summary**: Clear overview of total income, total expense, and net balance for the selected month.
3. **Transaction Inspection & Filtering**: Search notes and filter by category with instant feedback.
4. **Category Breakdown**: Understand spending distribution across categories.
5. **Account Management**: Track balances across cash, bank, credit cards, and other accounts.
6. **Data Portability**: One-click Excel export and secure database backup.

## Design Goals (Impeccable Craft)
- Transform the incumbent bland UI into a world-class, professional financial dashboard inspired by Linear and Stripe (Operate mode).
- Ensure flawless tabular number alignment, crisp color roles (emerald for income, rose for expense), refined card layouts, and intuitive form ergonomics.
